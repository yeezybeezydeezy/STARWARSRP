Where's my PAC file? (you can also drag it onto this box)
  1. In game, open the PAC editor and save your outfit under a name you'll recognise. That makes that name.txt. The outfit you spawn with is also kept as autoload.txt.
  2. PAC saves them here:
    C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\GarrysMod\garrysmod\data\pac3
    Click Merge into my PAC…, click the address bar at the top of the window that opens, paste, press Enter, then pick your file.
  3. Not there? Garry's Mod may be on another drive: in Steam, right-click Garry's Mod → Manage → Browse local files, then open garrysmod → data → pac3.
  4. The __backup folders in there are PAC's automatic backups, if you need an older version.
No files: copy and paste instead
  1. In game, wear your outfit and open the PAC editor. In the menu you load and save outfits from, pick to clipboard. (Not "copy uid": that only copies one part's ID.)
  2. Click in the box below and press Ctrl+V. Your helmet is found right away.
  3. Set up the visor, then click Copy merged PAC (next to Merge into my PAC).
  4. In PAC, open the top menu → load → load from clipboard, press Ctrl+V and confirm. Your outfit comes back with the visor in it.
You changed texture settings. Download the new .vtf, host it (see "Textures & files"), and paste its link into Visor texture link before downloading the outfit, or other players won't see the change.
How to use the files
  1. Click Download outfit. If you changed the visor's look, it builds and uploads the new texture first and links it for you.
  2. Put the .txt in GarrysMod\garrysmod\data\pac3\ and load it from pac → load, then click Wear.
  3. Already have a PAC? Use Merge into my PAC… first: the visor goes into your helmet's own material and your custom paint stays.
  4. To test a texture only you can see: download the .vtf, put it in garrysmod\materials\pac_visor\ and set Texture source to Local test file.
  5. Changes still need staff approval before you wear them on the server.
