How to use the files
  1. Click Download outfit. If you changed the visor's look, it builds and uploads the new texture first and links it for you.
  2. Put the .txt in GarrysMod\garrysmod\data\pac3\ and load it from pac → load, then click Wear.
  3. Already have a PAC? Use Merge into my PAC… first: the visor goes into your helmet's own material and your custom paint stays.
  4. To test a texture only you can see: download the .vtf, put it in garrysmod\materials\pac_visor\ and set Texture source to Local test file.
  5. Changes still need staff approval before you wear them on the server.
